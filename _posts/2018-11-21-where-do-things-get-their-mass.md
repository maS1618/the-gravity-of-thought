Okay. So where do things get their mass? I don't know. I haven't even started researching yet. It's probably gonna be something about how energy constantly keeps changing into mass, which, being only an illusion, doesn't really exist at all (alright, I might have researched a little bit). Anyway, while reading through all sorts of theories and formulae about our universe, I noticed something. No matter how many theories we make, in depth, all our questions come down to one basic answer: :"I don't know", and all our answers come down to one basic question :”what is the point of everything?” We don't know. All these years science has been a quest to find one unified theory- The Theory of Everything, and the most ‘plausible’ explanation is the vaguest theory imaginable- the string theory. You see, we've gotten so used to breaking science down into parts, we can't seem to make it whole again. And that's why we're here. So mix things up .Mold them into dreams like clay. Break them down again, start over. Fix them together like jigsaw puzzles, and shift them around like clothes in a messed up room.   
  
I'm talking bio, physics, chemistry, philosophy, psychology. If math doesn't work, use brute force. Trial and error. Just don't let the clothes get out of the room, or into closets. Keep them in sight, and very messed up. You'll eventually learn where to find them.  
  
(No, this was not an insight into the messiness of your room either. If you’re still confused about where this leading, the answer is nowhere. It’s a perpetual maze of meaning, where answers don’t occur in chronological order, like science.)
