Artist: Divyanshi Tulsian  
_This is not a work of fiction.   _  
  
Are you an act of God?  
  
Multiple water droplets on branches reach down to you - do you respond in the right encryption?  
  
To start climbing _after_ you are through- on top of a bookshelf - where do you climb to, now (irrelevant answer). Does it matter that the beams that hold up this architecture are geometrically infinite? (relevant question)  
  
The street lamp that does not need a street, the window not constructed around a view (in fact too high from a lower perspective to exhibit anything but the sky), the tree that is a self sustaining ecosystem -- these are unobstructed beings, like us.  
  
The indestructible completeness of every single object in the room is indicative of a place that is not 'somewhere'. It is everywhere.  
  
Addressed to the artist:  
  
You have never left the gravity of your room's floor, but you know what life looks like from the ceiling.  
  
When I was a child (darkness - unasleep) I was pulled out of confining matter and lifted toward a place that was everything.  
  
There were trees, a language marginally legible but completely understood at first glance -- the struggle to read on still, as information and reality let me in. I wasn't absorbing knowledge -- knowledge was absorbing me. And then dropped abruptly, nonchalantly back into my body, as if being told- "Fine. Return to that if you intend to, but don't expect sympathy".  
  
{Perhaps the reason I'm constantly writhing}  
  
Since then there is an all encompassing ache that holds us - it's not something that will leave while a room's floor still has significance.  
  
But this sketch reminds (in fractal moments, forgets) us how it feels when one's soul isn't in pain.  
  


_The fractal boundary of this smooth chaos is  _

_rhythm  longing for rhythm_

_The rhythm of life  _

_getting used to manipulation._

__Me_
