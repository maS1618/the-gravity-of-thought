Take a hammer- find a delicate piece of furniture- bring it down as hard as you can- feel the wood shatter through your thoughts, like you would love to break the whole world?  


Catharsis.   


  


Rain down on your garden every day, whether they need it or not- you do. You need to know that they're getting enough water, but have you ever tried leaving it wanting? Heard the dry leaves rustle in anticipation when you finally take out the watering can (guilt and love waltzing ruefully in your brain) and found that this is better. Petrichor- the aroma of water meeting dry ground.

  


Find a question you like the look of (Seriously, it should look good), and think about it. Think about it if it takes effort, if you're bored, if it's starting to look irritating, if you wanna go home and sleep. Go home. Think about it. Sleep. Wake up. Think. Repeat. Wonder how it's real, how it's related to the universe, why it affects you, why it shouldn't. Wonder why the hell it looked so pretty in the first place. 

Oh and tell me when you find the answer- when you're done celebrating how it made you feel. Love- the phenomenon you explain after a helluva lot of crazy thinking.

  


Name all the people you remember meeting. Call them what you want- the research guy, the yellow-haired pianist, the three-year-old who bit my hand, the suspicious looking lady that never returned my pen, the girl on the swing on the tree on the weird planet. Would you want to meet any of them again, or are accidental fragments of life more beautiful than the whole? Does it scare _everyone_ to think that things might not be as lovely as they seem? Nostalgia- selective memory of what we've experienced in the past- and thankfully, never will again. 

  


Take a pen out. Do it. 

Seriously, find a pen, find a paper, do as I say.

  


Write your name on it. Not your name as chosen by someone else- your nostalgic name- the first thing you thought of when you met yourself. Now write like the wind. Nonsensical, stupid, intelligent, beautiful, ugly stuff. Write everything, anything, anything at all- you don't have to make sentences- don't worry about punctuation, put down whatever the heck you feel like throwing out or preserving. You're not allowed to break in between- write without stopping till your hands hurt, write more if no one has declared you crazy yet, keep going even if they have. 

Go on, I'll be here when you return.  

  


If you followed every instruction, then what you have before you is a work of art, even if it doesn't look like it yet. You'll interpret it in a few days, weeks, years. The human brain is influenced by everything it sees, reads, hears- but try and rush it like this, and it tells you exactly what it's thinking- without unnecessary punctuation, without considering it twice. 

  


Yeah I know how difficult this exercise is- but we pay for art- thankfully-like we do for life.
